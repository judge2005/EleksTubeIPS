Back up or restore flash

This browser can't connect to USB devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Save a full copy of your clock's existing firmware over USB before installing EleksTubeIPS, or restore a previous full backup.

1Before you start

  • Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Phones and most other browsers can't connect to USB devices.
  • Connect the clock to the computer with a USB cable that carries data, not just power.
  • If the main installer page is open in another tab, make sure it isn't connected to the clock—only one tab can use the USB port at a time.

2Connect your clock

Click Connect and choose your clock's serial port. The page will automatically detect your flash size and fall back to a slower baud rate if needed.

Some clocks have to be manually put into programming mode before connecting (e.g. the IPSTube clocks). Hold down the power button while you plug it in—the display will stay blank.

3Back up or restore

Download full backup

Reads the entire flash chip starting at 0x0 (4 MB or 8 MB depending on your clock) and saves it as a single .bin file.

Reading the full chip takes around 45–90 seconds. Keep the USB cable connected until the download finishes.

Restore full backup

Writes a previously saved full-chip .bin image back to 0x0, returning your clock to the exact state it was in when backed up.

Only select a full-flash backup file here, not a firmware-only update file.

Connect your clock in step 2 to turn on these buttons.
